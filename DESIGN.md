# Vehicle Expense Tracker - Design System

## Product Identity
A personal vehicle management companion - think of it as a well-organized glove compartment that tracks every expense, stores important documents, and never lets you miss a deadline.

## Design Style: Soft Card UI with Automotive Warmth

### Color Palette
- **Primary**: `#2563EB` (Trust Blue) - reliability, automotive industry standard
- **Background**: `#F8FAFC` (Cool Slate) - clean, professional
- **Surface**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) - high contrast cards
- **Accent**: `#F59E0B` (Amber) - urgency for reminders, fuel gauge warmth
- **Danger**: `#EF4444` (Red) - overdue alerts
- **Success**: `#10B981` (Emerald) - completed items
- **Text Primary**: `#0F172A` (Slate 900)
- **Text Secondary**: `#64748B` (Slate 500)
- **Shadow Tone**: `#2563EB` (blue-tinted shadows for depth)

### Typography
- Headers: Bold, 24-28px for page titles
- Stats: 32px, extra bold for key numbers (expense totals)
- Body: 14-16px regular
- Labels: 12px, muted color

### Core Visual Elements
- Cards with `borderRadius: 20`, soft blue-tinted shadows
- Category icons in colored circular containers (48x48, borderRadius: 14)
- Status badges with pill shape (borderRadius: 12)
- Generous padding (20-24px) for breathing room
- Tab bar with active indicator using primary blue

### Interaction Patterns
- Smooth card press feedback (scale 0.98)
- Modal forms for quick expense entry
- Color-coded category system (fuel=amber, maintenance=blue, insurance=purple, inspection=green)
